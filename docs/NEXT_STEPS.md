# Next steps (v0.2 and beyond)

v0.1 is a first, retrospective release: it shows the warning would have worked for the class of 2024. The next versions make it prospective and sturdier.

1. **Prospective warnings for classes that have not yet reached college.** Add the 2024 and 2025 SAT grade 11 files and ILEARN 2021–2022 grade 8 files (all listed on the IDOE data center) to publish predictions for the classes of 2025 and 2026, then score them when CHE releases those cohorts (2027, 2028). This is the real test of an early-warning tool.
2. **Annual refit protocol.** Each spring, when CHE releases a new cohort: refit on the two most recent cohorts, update the intercept from the statewide figure, report calibration on the newest cohort, then re-publish the map. Script it as `run_all.sh --refresh`.
3. **Feeder mapping.** Replace corporation-average grade 8 attribution with feeder-school weights (middle school → high school) where IDOE or districts publish feeder patterns.
4. **CRDC 2023–24 in full.** Add 2023–24 Algebra I and math enrollment when the Urban portal posts them (offerings are already used for the map).
5. **Partner validation.** Share the flagged list privately with two or three districts and record whether flags match their own knowledge; publish the agreement rate.
6. **Outcomes beyond the first year.** CHE also reports persistence and completion; test whether the same levers predict second-year persistence.
7. **Venue path.** EdArXiv preprint → AERA Open or Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis (research article) → a short practitioner brief for Indiana districts.
