How Indiana high schools' graduates fare in their first college year

Each dot is a public high school. The model predicts first-year college credits and GPA for the class of 2024 from signals the school could see while those students were still in high school: grade 7/8 math, grade 11 SAT math, Algebra I timing, course offerings, AP and dual credit. It was trained on the class of 2023 only, so the class-of-2024 comparison below is a genuine test.

How to read this

Outcomes are averages over the school's 2024 graduates who enrolled at an Indiana public college that fall (Indiana Commission for Higher Education, 2026 College-Going Dataset). Schools with fewer than 10 such enrollees, and adult-learner and correctional schools without reportable outcomes, are not shown.

A school is flagged when its predicted credits fall in the lowest fifth, or its predicted share earning fewer than 24 credits falls in the highest fifth, among schools with observed outcomes. A flag is a prompt to look closer, not a rating. The model explains roughly three-fifths of the differences between schools; the rest is unexplained, and it misses some schools by several credits.

The lever bars compare the school to the state median (vertical tick). They are associations across schools, not proof that changing one lever changes outcomes. Sources: CHE College-Going/Readiness datasets; IDOE ILEARN, ISTEP+ and SAT school files; U.S. Department of Education Civil Rights Data Collection and NCES Common Core of Data via the Urban Institute Education Data Portal. State outline is approximate.
